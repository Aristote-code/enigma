## Examples

### Variants

<ComponentPreview name="status-badge-variants" peekCode />

The `variant` prop controls the dot color: `online`, `offline`, `away`, `busy`, and `neutral` (the default). The `size` prop accepts `sm` and `md` (the default). The badge label is passed as `children`, and any other `div` attributes are forwarded to the root element.
